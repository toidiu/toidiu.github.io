I am passionate about privacy and security, and I have spent a lot of time thinking about it. When a
friend asked me to audit their software stack, I decided to document my mental model so that I could
share it more broadly.

For me, specific software choices are transient due to the changing nature of our industry. For this
reason, I have found it better to think in terms of "incentives" and to position my software stack
for adaptability. For example, my primary email is on my own domain so that I am not locked into any
single email provider.
