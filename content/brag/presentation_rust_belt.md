Early in my journey to learn Rust, I signed up to give a talk at Rust Belt Rust and share some
design principles I learned while building a personal project. This was my first ever conference
presentation, and I felt extremely nervous since there were many Rust experts in the room. However,
I remember talking to an attendee afterwards and further discussing how a design principle applied
more generally across software. So I think at least one person listened and potentially benefited
from my talk.
