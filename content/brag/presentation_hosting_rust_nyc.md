This one loosely fits the presentation theme. Back in 2018, two others and I helped relaunch the
Rust meetup in NYC. I took the opportunity because I wanted to learn Rust and wanted a community and
space to do it.

For about one year, I was the primary host of the Rust NYC meetup. I found speakers, hosts, and
sponsors for the meetup, and recorded the presentation (the ones around 2018). My favorite part was
meeting new Rust enthusiasts and the chats we had afterwards. The other members took over these
responsibilities at the end of the year since I was moving out of NYC.
